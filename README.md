### 🔭 Current research

**Spatial inequality in Iran with nighttime lights** (M.Sc. thesis, supervisor: Dr. Saeed Tajrishy)<br>
I built a 2012–2024 provincial panel for Iran's 31 provinces that combines VIIRS nighttime lights with provincial GDP,
census population and household expenditure (HIES). I use it to validate lights as a proxy for economic activity and to
measure inequality across provinces. Extending the series back to 1992 with DMSP data is the next step.
